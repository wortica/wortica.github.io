Delete your Originly data

Originly: AI Detector & Plagiarism Check · developer: Miloni Patel

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Originly and tap the settings icon (top right).
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete all my data.
  3. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Your account is deleted from our servers straight away, and the app removes your saved texts and starts fresh. To delete only your history, use Delete my checks instead.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email infowortica@gmail.com with the subject "Originly data deletion request". If you can, include your user ID (shown in Settings → Contact support). We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account and device record
  • Your check history and results, word balance, rewards and usage
  • Analytics events and crash reports linked to you
  • Your push-notification token

What is kept

  • Purchase records, for as long as the law requires for accounting.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that can't identify you.
  • A subscription itself lives with your Google Play account: deleting your data does not cancel it. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Data you never deleted is removed automatically after 2 years without using the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
